🔬 Expected Points (xP) Engine & FPL Rules Architecture

Transparent breakdown of how our algorithmic engine models points and adheres to official FPL game rules

📐

The Comprehensive xP Formula

xP = [ Base_Appearance + Form_Rating_Boost + Attacking_Potential + Defensive_Potential + Multi_Season_Track_Record ] × Fixture_Difficulty_Multiplier × Availability_Factor
  • Base Appearance (2.0 pts): Awarded for players starting and expected to play 60+ minutes.
  • Form Rating Boost: Scaled dynamically based on match ratings: (Rating - 6.0) × 1.1. Top rated players (>7.8) gain up to +2.0 xP form boost.
  • Attacking Potential (Directly driven by Bayesian Holistic xG & xA):
    • FWD: (Holistic xG/90 × 4.0) + (Holistic xA/90 × 3.0) + (Shots/90 × 0.10)
    • MID: (Holistic xG/90 × 5.0) + (Holistic xA/90 × 3.0) + (Big Chances/90 × 0.35)
    • DEF: (Holistic xG/90 × 6.0) + (Holistic xA/90 × 3.0) (Defenders receive 6 pts per goal in FPL).
  • Defensive Potential & Contributions: Clean sheet odds (4 pts for DEF/GKP, 1 pt for MID), goalkeeper save points (1 pt per 3 saves), and defensive contributions/90:
    • DEF: 10 defensive contributions award +2.0 points (DefActions/90 × 0.20).
    • MID: 12 defensive actions award +2.0 points (DefActions/90 × 0.167).
    • GKP: Saves award +1 point per 3 saves (Saves/90 ÷ 3.0).
    • Goals Conceded Deduction (GKP & DEF): Goalkeepers and defenders lose -1 point for every 2 goals conceded by their team while on the pitch. Modeled using Poisson distribution expectations E[⌊K/2⌋] = (2λ - 1 + e^(-2λ)) / 4 where λ is opponent-adjusted expected goals conceded.
  • Disciplinary Deductions: Penalizes bookings and reckless foul play: -(Yellow Cards/90 × 1.0) - (Red Cards/90 × 3.0) - (Fouls/90 × 0.10).
  • Multi-Season Track Record (60-65% weighting): Incorporates historical points per 90 and cumulative totals across multi-year past seasons. Anchors projections to proven multi-season pedigree (60-65% weight), strictly preventing early-season noise from hiding proven premium assets.
  • Fixture Multiplier (FDR): FDR 2 (Easy fixture) multiplies score by 1.14x; FDR 4 reduces to 0.84x; FDR 5 to 0.70x.
  • Availability Factor: 0% if injured/red-flagged; scaled to chance percentage (e.g. 75% or 50%) if doubtful.
📋

Official FPL Rules Enforced

  • Transfer Hit Penalty (-4 Points):

    Each extra transfer beyond your available Free Transfers incurs an immediate 4-point deduction. Our optimizer strictly accounts for this: it will only recommend a transfer beyond your free quota if the expected net gain exceeds 2.5 xP.

  • Goals Conceded Deduction (-1 Pt per 2 Goals Conceded):

    Goalkeepers and defenders lose 1 point for every 2 goals their team concedes while they are on the pitch. Our engine applies exact Poisson probability distribution expectations to deduct expected points for defensive concessions based on Team xGC and fixture difficulty.

  • Rolling Free Transfers:

    Saving a transfer rolls it forward into the next gameweek (up to 5 banked transfers in modern FPL). If your squad is healthy and no available move provides a net improvement in points or budget, the engine recommends ROLL TRANSFER to preserve transfer equity.

  • Club Limits (Max 3 per Team):

    You cannot select more than 3 players from any single Premier League club (e.g., max 3 Man City, max 3 Arsenal). Every transfer recommendation validates club quotas.

  • Starting XI Formation Constraints:

    Every active lineup must adhere to valid tactical shapes: 1 Goalkeeper, minimum 3 Defenders, minimum 2 Midfielders, and minimum 1 Forward.

  • Bank Budget Cap:

    All transfer options must strictly fit within your available bank balance (selling price + bank).
